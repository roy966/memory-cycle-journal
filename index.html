The Information (Qianer Liu), via Reuters and other wraps, reports that TSMC has started charting plans for 3D DRAM at the request of Nvidia, its largest customer. The effort is led by Hsu Kuo-chin, vice president for advanced packaging. Options under discussion include building new fabs or acquiring/converting an existing plant. There is no timeline, no capex decision, and no production date. TSMC and Nvidia did not immediately comment. Plans-only — a long-dated competitive option for memory incumbents, not near-term supply relief (The Information / Reuters via Investing.com / Techmeme / The Verge) Report.

Markets told two stories on Friday. In New York, chips bounced after Thursday’s wipeout: Micron closed at $690.00 (+5.12%), Sandisk $835.06 (+3.55%), Lam Research $200.44 (+8.23%), and the PHLX SOX rose 4.21% to 9,390.25. The Nasdaq Composite gained 1.09%. Bargain-hunting after Thursday’s SOX −12.25% / MU −17.46% / SNDK −22.26% — still far below Wednesday levels (Motley Fool; Exa).

In Asia, Friday’s official closes confirmed the damage and correct No. 31’s near-close prints. KOSPI finished at 5,059.69 (−6.02%) — not the provisional 5,042.25 (−6.34%). Samsung closed at ₩188,700 (−6.35%); SK hynix at ₩1,132,000 (−10.44%). The session low was 4,911.28 (−8.78%) after a Level-1 circuit breaker early. Foreigners net sold ₩2.77 trillion (SK hynix alone −₩1.924T). The won settled at ₩1,356.6 per dollar. Two-day wipeout: Samsung −13.44% / SK hynix −18.91% (Yonhap / Korea Herald). Tokyo’s Nikkei 225 fell 5.96% to 57,434.77 — its third-largest point drop on record (Japan Times).

Spot DRAM did not break lower again on DDR5: the 16Gb print stayed at $60.167 (−0.82%) as of Nov 6, 18:10 GMT+8 — unchanged from No. 31’s morning print. DDR4 16Gb edged to $80.815 (−1.06%) from $80.938. The 512Gb TLC wafer weekly remains $16.152 (−4.33% w/w) from Nov 4 Insights.

Our call: late upcycle; tight through 2027 / into 2028 — unchanged on supply fundamentals. Policy and sentiment risk still elevated (Asia Friday FINAL confirms; US Friday bounce ≠ cycle turn). NAND · China · Capex

YMTC Targets 60–70% Local Tools for a Fourth Fab

Reuters, citing people familiar, says China’s YMTC plans to source 60–70% of equipment locally for a planned fourth Wuhan factory aimed at 300-layer-plus data-center NAND, up from about 50% at its third fab. Construction is expected to begin next year; no orders have been placed. Localization color — not a near-term supply increment (Reuters via Investing.com / Techmeme) Report.
