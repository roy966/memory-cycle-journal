Seoul Memory Stocks Rebound as Nvidia Opens a Probe Into Its CEO

Samsung Electronics and SK hynix led a Monday rebound in Seoul after Friday’s 6% crash, even as Nvidia, their largest HBM customer, said its board had opened an independent investigation into reports that CEO Jensen Huang was the target of a foreign intelligence effort. Seoul said contracted Nvidia GPU deliveries should proceed without disruption. DRAM spot was flat.

Specialty DRAM · Company comments

Winbond Sees Q4 Memory Prices Still Rising, Plans EUV by 2030

Winbond president Chen Pei-ming said memory prices will keep rising in the fourth quarter and revenue should grow again, but by less than in earlier quarters. Phone, PC and TV demand is still correcting. Some customers are holding sales up by raising prices while volumes and utilization fall; others are cutting memory content. He said the current rally should not be assumed to keep going up. As majors shrink DDR3, DDR4 and LPDDR4, auto customers are seeking long-term supply. Winbond’s 14nm node will come in non-EUV and EUV versions, with EUV targeted for 2030. ASML has promised the first scanner in about two years, and later tools in about three and a half. A recent NT$70bn-plus Kaohsiung approval covers only part of Module B (MoneyDJ, Nov 9).

Capex · Balance sheets

Samsung and SK Hynix Sit on ₩329 Trillion of Cash as Capex Climbs

Samsung held ₩214.8 trillion of cash at the end of the third quarter and SK hynix ₩114.5 trillion. That is ₩329.3 trillion combined, up ₩51.3 trillion in a quarter, with net cash of ₩197.6 trillion and ₩98.7 trillion. Samsung plans a record ₩84.4 trillion of capex this year, about 60% above last year’s ₩52.7 trillion, of which ₩76.8 trillion goes to chips. SK hynix plans to run M15X in Cheongju at full capacity by year-end for HBM4 and start the first Yongin fab next year. It will lift investment “significantly” in 2027, timing spend to demand and profitability (Korea JoongAng Daily, Nov 9). Separately, Financial News notes that “sold out” LTAs do not fix delivery timing: customers that delay acceptance can shift quarterly revenue even if volumes hold (FnNews, Nov 8).
