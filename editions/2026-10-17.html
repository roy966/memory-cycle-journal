China supply · Margins

CXMT’s Profit Stalls: 9-Month Guide Implies Q3 Net Up Only 1–4% on 15–20% More Revenue

CXMT pre-announced 9M26 net profit of RMB131.0–132.5B on revenue of RMB265–270B (unaudited, forecast notice 2026-014; Reuters headline, CTOL Digital). That implies Q3 net profit of RMB53.4–54.9B, +1.0–3.9% q/q after +113% in Q2, while implied revenue rose 15–20% q/q. Implied Q3 net margin falls to 44.6–47.9% from 53.1%. Price or ramp costs? The full Q3 report should tell (CTOL). Confirmed Cycle signal

Sell side · Shortage

Wall Street Pushes Back: Goldman Sees Korean Memory Priced for a 2028 Downturn at Under 1× Book; Micron Says HBM Booked Through 2027

Goldman (Investing.com): Samsung, SK hynix, MU, SNDK, Kioxia and CXMT are −11% to −26% since Oct 10, with >$700B wiped; P/B on 2028E is 0.9× for both Korean names; “still a significant technology gap” in advanced DRAM and HBM, though few near-term catalysts. TrendForce’s Oct 16 HBM bulletin sees HBM undersupplied through 2028; MK cites Kyobo’s HBM share of DRAM capacity rising from 27% (2025) to 40% (2027). Verdict
Late upcycle; tight through 2027, unchanged, with a small nudge looser on 2028 supply. Korea’s Friday bounce (Kospi +2.05%; Samsung +3.3%, SKH +0.2%) failed in New York: SOX −3.83% with all 30 down and MU −5.63% to $878.84 (about −14.6% on the week), as AI-financing stress (CoreWeave CDS 1,117bp) piled onto the CXMT scare. Fundamentals still read tight (Micron HBM booked through 2027; TrendForce: HBM short through 2028). New caution flag: CXMT’s implied Q3 profit is flat q/q on 15–20% more revenue, and TrendForce describes spot buyers as wait-and-see.
