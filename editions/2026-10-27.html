What this shows: memory stocks rose in New York on Monday and in Seoul on Tuesday. Spot DRAM was flat. Two forecasters put fourth-quarter contract prices up 10–20%, and the JPM and TrendForce figures agree. Korean and Japanese prices are Tuesday closes (15:30 local, 08:30 IST). Sources: Yahoo Finance, TrendForce, J.P. Morgan via X, Seagate, Korea Times.

Bank of America kept its Buy rating and $1,550 target on Micron after meetings with senior management. Analyst Vivek Arya says supply looks tighter in 2027–28. Customer agreements now run for several years, and talks on 2031 supply are under way. About 35% of sales are under such agreements, and the aim is 50% by 2030 (Investing.com, 24/7 Wall St). The note also says 1δ DRAM ramps in volume in the second half of 2027 and that buybacks step up from Dec. 9. Micron rose 2.98% to $903.70.

Chip stocks rebounded Monday as Anthropic’s IPO filing revived the AI trade: the SOX gained 2.82%, and Western Digital (+3.63%), Seagate (+2.18%) and Sandisk (+1.42%) followed (CNBC). J.P. Morgan, via @pequityresearch, sees fourth-quarter contract prices up 13% for DRAM and 18% for NAND, in line with TrendForce. Spot was flat. In Seoul, KOSPI closed up 3.09% Tuesday after a buy sidecar at 10:13 KST, as foreigners turned net buyers after Monday’s lowest close since April 6 (Hankyung, Seoul Economic Daily).

Our call: late upcycle, tight through 2027, unchanged. A touch firmer on how long agreements last and on packaging tightness, while the rally rests on fragile positioning. Cycle signal: LTAs lengthen

HBM · Packaging

Nvidia Lowers Vera Rubin’s HBM4 Bandwidth 13% to Ease Supply

Nvidia’s Vera Rubin datasheet now lists HBM4 bandwidth per GPU at 19.2 TB/s instead of 22 TB/s, and the NVL72 rack at 1,400 TB/s instead of 1,580 TB/s. HBM capacity stays at 288GB (ETNews). That implies about 9.4–9.6 Gbps per pin instead of about 11.7, which lowers the bar for yields and qualification at all three suppliers, which should help Micron and narrow the leader’s premium. ZDNet Korea reports Samsung and SK hynix will ship 8-high HBM4 to Nvidia from about 2Q27. The bottleneck is moving downstream: Report TSMC is weighing CoWoS price rises of up to 20% (Commercial Times via TrendForce; TSMC has not confirmed). Applied Materials said Monday it will lift its Besi stake to about 20% to work on hybrid bonding. More supply per wafer · packaging tight
