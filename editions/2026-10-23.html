Western Digital Raises Its Outlook and Sees More Price Rises, but Chip Stocks Have Their Worst Day Since June

Storage prices are still rising and customer visibility runs to 2030. Investors sold anyway, on AI-funding fears and $113 oil.

Sources: WDC release and call (Investing.com); closes from StreetInsider, Swingfolio, Yonhap.

Western Digital reported September-quarter revenue of $4.38 billion, up 55% from a year earlier and above the ~$4.14 billion consensus. Gross margin was 58.1% and EPS $4.64. Exabytes shipped rose 22% to 248, and price per terabyte rose 28%. December guidance is $5.1 billion ±$100 million with a 60–61% gross margin and EPS of $5.80 ±$0.20. CFO Kris Sennesael: “We continue to operate in a very strong, favorable pricing environment.” Management sees room for more price-per-terabyte increases over the next three to four quarters, and visibility with its top seven customers out to 2030–31. Capex runs above its 4–6% range, on heads and media, not unit capacity.

The tape ignored it. The SOX fell 6.72% to 10,731.98, its worst day since June, now 26% below its June 22 record. Lam fell 11.76%, Sandisk 8.51%, Seagate 7.86% and Micron 6.26%. Revived doubts about AI spending drove the selling, and Brent jumped 4% to $113.17. Jensen Huang told CNBC he does not expect a memory glut by 2029: “One of the things that’s really great about memory is that you can always use more of it.”

Our call: late upcycle, tight through 2027, unchanged; firmer on storage pricing and visibility, weaker on the tape, and NAND still the soft spot. Cycle signal: fundamentals firm, flows weak

Korea · Capital markets

SK Hynix Halts the Solidigm IPO, and Seoul Claws Back Most of an Early Rout

Before Friday’s open, SK hynix said Solidigm had stopped its pre-IPO and IPO review “after taking capital market conditions and other factors into consideration.” It needs the same money for a new US fab and is exploring other options (SK hynix). After Thursday’s 3.52% drop to 5,533.77, the Kospi opened −2.36% and fell as far as −4.12%. By 13:42 KST it was down only 0.71% at 5,494.56 despite ₩1T+ of foreign selling. At 13:58 KST SK hynix was up 2.62% at ₩1,255,000, while Samsung was down 0.93%. In Tokyo, Kioxia fell as much as 9.64% to ¥11,380, a six-month low (Nikkei). Overhang removed

Taiwan · Labor and weather

Micron Offers Taiwan Staff a Quarterly Profit Bonus as Typhoon Delays Union Talks

The Taoyuan union’s 24-hour strike ended at 09:00 Friday Taiwan time, and the second round of talks moved to Tuesday, Oct. 27 because Typhoon Champi shut much of Taiwan (Liberty Times). Micron has offered a new quarterly profit-sharing bonus, based on quarterly net income minus next-quarter forecast capex and paid half in stock and half in cash. It starts in fiscal 2027 and covers staff worldwide. The union wants 15% of global operating profit (Taipei Times). Cost up, supply risk low

China · Prices

CXMT’s Profit Growth Stalls as Commodity DRAM Cools; NAND Quotes Keep Slipping

CXMT guided third-quarter revenue up 15–20% from the second quarter, to ¥114.7–119.7 billion, but net income up only 1.0–3.8%, to ¥53.4–54.9 billion (The Inference). YMTC is testing 16Gb LPDDR5 at its Fab 2 and plans a 4F² part for Fab 3 (Counterpoint via Wccftech). Spot: DDR5 16Gb rose 0.16% to $61.20, and DDR4 16Gb fell 0.30% to $82.87 (TrendForce). DRAMeXchange says NAND quotes are “weak and continue to decline.” DRAM flat, NAND soft

Verdict
Late upcycle; tight through 2027, unchanged. Firmer on storage (WDC price/TB accelerating, visibility to 2030–31; Solidigm IPO overhang gone); offsets are flat DRAM spot, falling NAND quotes and stalled CXMT profit growth. The tape, not memory data, is the weak link.
