SK Hynix Jumps 4.4% and Micron Gains 2.5% as Chip Stocks Steady After Thursday’s Rout

Memory led a cautious bounce on Friday, helped by the scrapped Solidigm listing. Storage stocks were still sold, and oil near $117 kept a lid on the rest of the market.

Seoul opened Friday down 2.36% and finished up 0.30% at 5,550.30. SK hynix closed up 4.42% at ₩1,277,000 after opening lower, and Samsung rose 0.47% to ₩215,500. Daishin’s Lee Kyoung-min: SK hynix gained “as sentiment improved somewhat following news that Solidigm’s IPO plans had been scrapped” (Yonhap). Institutions bought a net ₩596.1 billion of SK hynix while foreigners sold ₩559.7 billion (JoongAng Daily). Breadth was poor, with 716 decliners to 173 advancers, and the Kosdaq fell 1.62%. Kioxia rose 1.27% to ¥12,755, a day after its six-month low (Nikkei).

In New York the SOX rose 0.44% to 10,779.46, “a pause after Thursday’s 30-for-30 selloff” (Cappelleri). Micron gained 2.46% to $877.55. Storage did not join in: Sandisk fell 1.82%, Seagate 1.81% and Western Digital 2.97%. The S&P 500 slipped 0.52% as fresh Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia lifted WTI 3.5% to $102.96, and the 10-year yield sat near 5%. LYNX said the scrapped Solidigm IPO removes an overhang of new NAND capacity, a plus for Micron and Sandisk (Investing.com), and Jensen Huang again rejected glut fears (DIGITIMES).

Our call: late upcycle, tight through 2027, unchanged; a touch tighter on supply discipline (Solidigm, Micron skipping 4F²), with the tape steadier but not repaired and storage still sold. Cycle signal: fundamentals firm, flows stabilizing

Korea · HBM share

Bernstein Says Samsung Passed SK Hynix in HBM Revenue Last Quarter

In an Oct. 22 note, Bernstein estimates Samsung’s third-quarter HBM revenue at $8.7 billion, up 33% from the second quarter, against $7.6 billion for SK hynix, up 11%. If right, Samsung has overtaken SK hynix in HBM revenue. The estimate is built from Korean export data, not company disclosures, so both companies’ calls next week are the test: SK hynix at 02:00 IST on Wednesday and Samsung at 03:00 IST on Thursday (Investing.com). For the cycle, a share shift matters less than total HBM supply; for the stocks, it bears on SK hynix’s premium. Estimate · share shift

Micron · Technology and labor

Micron Drops 4F² DRAM, and Its Taoyuan Workers Reject a Revised Bonus Offer

After its Oct. 21 webinar, Micron has officially abandoned the 4F² cell, citing “no viable scaling path,” ETNews reported. It will extend 6F², start 1δ mass production in Boise in the second half of 2027, add wafer bonding at 1ε and move to 3D DRAM between 1ζ and 1η. Fewer node transitions mean less disruptive bit growth from Micron, while CXMT is pushing 4F². In Taiwan, 1,633 of 1,637 voting members of the Taoyuan union, about 70% of its 2,300+ members, rejected Micron’s profit-sharing proposal and warned of “further collective action.” Talks resume Oct. 27 (Straits Times, CNA). Micron makes 50–60% of its chips in Taiwan. Supply discipline · labor risk

Policy · China

Washington Proposes Wider Curbs on U.S. Investment in Chinese Chipmaking

Treasury issued a proposed rule under the COINS Act on Oct. 22 that would require notice of, or prohibit, US investments in Chinese firms working in semiconductors and microelectronics, AI, quantum, supercomputing and hypersonics, with biotechnology added. Final rules will come later (US Treasury). CXMT and YMTC fund their expansion mostly at home, so the near-term effect on Chinese memory supply is small; the direction of policy is the signal. Separately, DIGITIMES reports Nvidia left cooperation with CXMT open. Policy · watch
