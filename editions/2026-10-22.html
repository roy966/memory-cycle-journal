Lam Sees 2027 Tool Spending Above $200 Billion, Led by DRAM, as Micron Sees Two More Years of Shortage

Both argue for a long upcycle. But a DRAM-led jump in tool spending is also how the next glut starts.

Sources: Lam Research press release and call transcript (Investing.com); Micron via Stocktwits/Yahoo Finance; after-hours prices from Investing.com and public.com (aggregators, secondary).

Lam Research reported record September-quarter revenue of $8.22 billion, up 22% from June, with a 52.4% non-GAAP gross margin, and guided December to $8.60 billion ±$400 million. CEO Tim Archer put 2026 wafer-fab equipment spending in the “mid-$150 billion range” and gave a preliminary 2027 outlook of “more than $200 billion… percentage growth led by DRAM, followed by leading-edge foundry logic and NAND.” Customer visibility extends “through 2028,” but cleanroom space and component supply will cap 2027 deliveries. Ten new tier-1 fabs open next year. Memory was a record 46% of systems revenue, and each point of NAND bits shifted to SLC-class devices needs about 2% more wafer capacity.

Micron said at its technology webinar that the shortage will last at least two years; CTO Scott DeBoer said, “we actually don’t see a time when it comes into balance,” as HBM and vertical HBM use more wafers per bit. It called rival 4F² DRAM “a bit of a myth.” Micron closed at $913.67, about 40% below its June record.

Our call: late upcycle, tight through 2027, unchanged; a touch tighter on duration, with a louder 2028 supply flag. Cycle signal: long tight, capex rising

Taiwan · Labor

Micron Signs a Deal With Its Largest Taiwan Union; Taoyuan Officers Strike for a Day

Micron’s Taichung union, at its largest Taiwan site, signed a labor-management agreement and both sides withdrew from Thursday’s mediation; terms were not disclosed (Reuters). The Taoyuan union said Micron “failed to provide specific figures” on profit-sharing, and 11 union officers began a 24-hour strike at 09:00 Taiwan time Thursday (04:00 IDT). The union will poll members on the company’s proposal (CNA). TrendForce says Taoyuan makes front-end DRAM wafers while Taichung handles advanced DRAM, HBM and packaging, so a one-day action is “likely limited.” Cycle signal: supply risk easing

Korea · Flows

Seoul Gives Back Wednesday’s Rally as ₩55 Trillion of Chipmaker Buybacks Run Out

The Kospi rose 3.29% to 5,735.66 on Wednesday (Yonhap Infomax), then reversed: at 11:20 KST Thursday it was down 2.25% at 5,605.54, Samsung −3.36% and SK hynix −3.63% (Yonhap), both about −4% near 13:00 (Hankyung). Intraday, not closes. SK hynix finished its ₩40.43 trillion buyback on Oct. 21, a month early; Samsung’s ~₩15 trillion ended Oct. 6 (MK). KB puts them at 3.3× and 2.7× earnings. Flows weak, cheap

China · Supply

CXMT Approves a ¥34.9 Billion R&D Plan and Promises 4F² Server DRAM This Year

CXMT shareholders approved ¥34.9 billion for advanced DRAM R&D and back-end investment. It estimates 1–3Q revenue of ¥265–270 billion (more than 7× a year earlier) and plans 4F² server DRAM “within the year,” with no specs (Global Economic). Counterpoint, relayed on X, says YMTC is researching 4F² too Unconfirmed. 2028 supply risk

In brief · GTC Berlin and spot prices

Nvidia Ships Vera Rubin AI Factories by Truck; DDR5 Spot Firm, NAND Soft

GTC Berlin. Nvidia’s DSX Pod puts seven Vera Rubin racks in a truck-shippable container, cutting time to serve tokens from six months to 14 days; deliveries start early 2027 (CNBC). Jensen Huang said Nvidia’s content per gigawatt is “now $40-plus billion” and “going to keep going up.” No new HBM figures. Demand strong

Spot. DDR5 16Gb rose 0.38% to $61.10, with buyers unable to secure allocation; DDR4 16Gb was flat at $83.117. NAND 512Gb TLC wafers fell 1.28% to $18.592 in the week of Oct. 19 on weak module-house demand and high inventory (TrendForce). DRAM firm, NAND soft

Verdict
Late upcycle; tight through 2027, unchanged. A touch tighter on duration: Micron sees at least two more years of shortage with no balance point in sight, and Lam’s customers have visibility through 2028. The 2028 supply flag is louder: DRAM-led 2027 tool spending above $200 billion and CXMT’s 4F² push. The tape is the weak link: Seoul −2% to −4% intraday after Wednesday’s +3.29%, with ₩55 trillion of buybacks finished.
