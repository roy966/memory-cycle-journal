SK Hynix Tops ₩100 Trillion in Quarterly Sales and Says Memory Stays Short Through 2030

Third-quarter sales and operating profit beat forecasts. The company says long-term deals with 14 customers cover more than half of its 2027–28 capacity and it will return about ₩100 trillion this year. Seoul jumped back above 6,000.

What this shows: SK hynix beat forecasts on sales and operating profit by about 6–9%, and Korean memory shares and Kioxia rose sharply. US memory stocks rose Tuesday before Seagate’s report, and Seagate fell after the close. Spot DRAM was flat. Korean and Japanese prices are Wednesday closes (15:30 local, 08:30 IST). Sources: SK hynix, CNBC (LSEG), Yahoo Finance, TrendForce, Seagate.

SK hynix reported third-quarter sales of ₩104.6 trillion, up 32% from the second quarter and the first quarter above ₩100 trillion. Operating profit was ₩83.3 trillion, an 80% margin. LSEG consensus was ₩98.3 trillion and ₩76.7 trillion (CNBC). Net profit fell to about ₩39.8 trillion after currency and Kioxia valuation losses. DRAM prices rose by a high-20s percentage from the second quarter and NAND by a mid-20s percentage. The company expects fourth-quarter DRAM shipments up by a mid-20s percentage and HBM4 to overtake HBM3E (company release, Moneycontrol).

Management said long-term agreements with 14 customers now cover more than half of 2027 and 2028 capacity, and it expects supply to stay short through 2030. It sees 2027 demand growth in the mid-20s percent for DRAM and the high teens for NAND, and capital spending “significantly” higher next year. Returns for 2026 total about ₩100 trillion, including a new ₩20 trillion buyback and a ₩20,000-a-share dividend, above the ₩60 trillion Korean press had reported. It also shelved the Solidigm IPO. Seoul rallied: KOSPI closed at 6,012.44, up 6.63%, back above 6,000; SK hynix rose 10.9% to ₩1,487,000, Samsung 9.0% to ₩242,000, SK Square 11.2% and Kioxia 13.8% in Tokyo (Yonhap, Aju Press). Management also said PC and phone volumes are falling as memory prices rise.

Our call: late upcycle, tight through 2027 and now booked into 2028. Slightly tighter than yesterday on how far ahead capacity is sold. The offsets are slower fourth-quarter price gains, larger 2027 capex budgets and China’s first HBM3E. Cycle signal: capacity sold years ahead

Storage · Hard drives

Seagate Beats and Guides Above Forecasts, but the Stock Slips After Hours

Seagate’s fiscal first-quarter revenue rose 61% to $4.23 billion. Gross margin was 57.8%, up from 52.7% last quarter, and EPS was $7.68, against a guide of $4.10 billion and $7.30. It guided the December quarter to $4.8 billion and $9.20 a share; the Street was near $4.53 billion and $8.58. Management said the “vast majority” of nearline exabyte supply is allocated under long-term agreements through calendar 2028, some through 2030. Exabyte shipments grew 21% from a year earlier, slower than 34% last quarter. The shares closed up 3.67% at $667.85 and then fell about 6–7% after hours (CNBC, Investing.com). Beat, raise · growth slows

Supply · China and U.S. capacity

China’s CXMT Moves Into HBM3E at Low Yields as Solidigm Floats a $45 Billion U.S. Plant

CXMT plans limited HBM3E production within about two months, with yields of 20–30% against about 70% at SK hynix, and performance closer to HBM3. It has sent samples to Chinese AI-chip designers (Reuters, sources). Separately, SK hynix’s Solidigm is in talks with the Commerce Department on a $45 billion US NAND plant, with New York a candidate. That is tied to approval for upgrading its Dalian, China plant (Bloomberg). Report · not confirmed by the companies Both are new supply, but neither adds meaningful bits before 2028. Read them alongside SK hynix’s higher 2027 capex: they set up the next downturn, not this one. 2028 supply risk

Prices · Labor

DRAM Price Gains Slow in the Fourth Quarter, and Micron’s Taiwan Talks End Without a Deal

Digitimes reports that fourth-quarter DRAM price growth has slowed sharply. At the same time, shortages are worsening: suppliers are restricting shipments, and the headline puts the supply squeeze at up to 50%. Allocation now matters more than price. TrendForce on Oct. 20 still saw fourth-quarter DRAM prices up about 20% from the third quarter. Spot was flat Wednesday: DDR5 16Gb at $61.33 and DDR4 16Gb up 0.36% at $82.92. In Taiwan, the second round of talks between Micron and its Taoyuan union ended Tuesday without agreement. A third session is tentatively set for Nov. 6 (Liberty Times). Momentum slows · labor watch
